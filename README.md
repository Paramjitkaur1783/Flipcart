# Flipkart Login Procedure

This guide explains how to log in to your Flipkart account.

## Steps to Login

1. **Open Flipkart Website or App**  
   Go to [https://www.flipkart.com](https://www.flipkart.com) in your web browser or open the Flipkart mobile app on your device.

2. **Click on the Login/Register Button**  
   At the top-right corner of the homepage, click on **Login** or **Sign In**.

3. **Enter Your Mobile Number or Email**  
   On the login page, enter your registered mobile phone number or email address in the input field.

4. **Enter Your Password**  
   Input your Flipkart password in the password field.

5. **Click on the Login Button**  
   After filling the credentials, click on the **Login** button to access your account.

6. **Access Your Account**  
   After successful login, you will be redirected to the Flipkart homepage where you can start shopping.
